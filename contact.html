Contact Us / Request Data

The following are the official channels through which you may contact the Philippine Statistics Authority or submit requests for statistical data, information, and related services.

Ways on how to Contact Us

Datasets

Download datasets at: OpenSTAT, CountrySTAT Philippines, Decent Work Statistics – Philippines, and Philippine Food Security Information System.

PSA Publications

View the catalog of PSA Publications at: https://library.psa.gov.ph

Microdata

View and download Microdata/Public Use Files with metadata from PSA surveys, censuses, and statistical reports through the PSA Data Archive Catalog at: https://psada.psa.gov.ph/index.php/home

Ways on how to request data from PSA:

Fill out the online Data Request Form (https://psa.gov.ph/content/data-request-form).

Contact the PSA Information Section at info@psa.gov.ph, and provide details on your data request.

Visit the PSA Library at 9/F PSA Headquarters PSA Complex, East Avenue Diliman, Quezon City.

Send a letter requesting for dataset addressed to the PSA National Statistics.

Claire Dennis S. Mapa, Ph.D.

Undersecretary
National Statistician and Civil Registrar General

PSA will respond to your data request within three (3) working days either clarify or address your data request.
Please be advised however that the length of time needed to completely address your request will vary depending on the availability of data.
