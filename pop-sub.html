Population and Vital Statistics

Population and Vital Statistics (PVS) presents data on the size, composition, and characteristics of the population and vital events the occur in the country. These statistics support planning, policy-making, and research for social and economic development.

Categories

Explore the vital events and population data available in each category.

Population

Data on the total population size, population growth, density, and distribution by age, sex, and geographic area.

Birth

Statistics on live birth including number of births, birth rates, age of mother and other birth-related characteristics.

Death

Data on deaths including number of deaths, death rates, causes of death, age, sex, and other demographic details.

Marriage

Statistics on marriages including number of marriages, marriage rates, age of couple, and type of ceremony.
